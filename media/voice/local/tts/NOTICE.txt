acoustic_encoder.onnx, acoustic_decoder.onnx, and vocoder.onnx are modified
from the v1b_16k/ files at that revision. Large finite float32 initializers are
stored as float16, with constant Cast nodes to restore float32 before inference.
Model computation and input and output tensor types remain float32 where the
source uses float32. The upstream and packaged SHA-256 hashes are in model.json.
scripts/local-speech/repack-prime-weights.py reproduces the three models from
the pinned upstream files. Python and conversion tools are only needed to
rebuild the weights. Normal installation and offline inference use the
packaged ONNX files directly.

meta.json is unmodified from v1b_16k/. symbol_table.json is unmodified from
scripts/symbol_table.json. The FastSpeech-frame expansion algorithm in
scripts/local-speech/prime.cjs is a port of PrimeTTS scripts/synth_from_text.py.
The shipped files do not include a reference WAV or any training data.

The upstream model card documents Inflect-Nano-v1 (Apache-2.0) as the v1
training base, OpenBMB VoxCPM2 (Apache-2.0) as the v1 teacher and Mozilla
Common Voice zh-TW (CC0) as the source for the v1 reference speaker. The
specific Common Voice clip and release are not identified in the model card.
https://huggingface.co/owensong/Inflect-Nano-v1
https://huggingface.co/openbmb/VoxCPM2
https://commonvoice.mozilla.org/datasets

The Mandarin frontend maps pinyin-pro syllables to PrimeTTS's Bopomofo symbols.
The unmodified pinyin-pro 3.29.5 CommonJS distribution and its MIT license
are included in scripts/local-speech/pinyin/. No neural G2PW model is included or required.
https://github.com/zh-lx/pinyin-pro/blob/main/LICENSE
